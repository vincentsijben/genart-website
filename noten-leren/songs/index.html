This page can't convert those itself. Use the local helper in the convert/ folder:

  • Screenshot or photo of sheet music, or a PDF: copy the screenshot to the clipboard (Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4) and double-click Paste and convert.command → MusicXML.
  • Audio or YouTube: copy the link or put the file in convert/inbox/ and double-click Convert.command → MIDI.
